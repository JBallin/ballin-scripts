# Research evidence and decision template

**Audience:** Maintainers using the [interview and trial guide](user-research.md).

This is a blank template, not research findings. Copy the session form into a
private document or spreadsheet. Publish only a reviewed, sanitized synthesis.
Keep names, contact details, employers/clients, recordings/transcripts, destination
identities/URLs, local paths, snapshot contents, and sensitive setup details out
of public notes. Omit distinctive combinations or quotes that could identify
someone. Pseudonyms alone do not make evidence anonymous.
